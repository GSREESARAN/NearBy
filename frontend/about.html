Our Story

Built for your City.
By people who love it.

NearBy was born from a simple observation. your favourite local shops weren't on any delivery app. We decided to fix that.

We started with one simple idea

Every small city has amazing local shops, the best biryani hotel, the trusted medical store, the kirana shop that knows your family by name. But none of them are on Zomato, Swiggy or Blinkit.

NearBy bridges that gap. We're not trying to be Swiggy. We're trying to be your city's own delivery service personal, fast, and truly local.

Our delivery boys know every street, every shortcut, and every shop in your city. That's our unfair advantage.
